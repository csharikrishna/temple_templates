Sanctum Pilgrimage Etiquette & Dress Code

Traditional Attire

Men: Dhoti, Kurta, or formal trousers with shirt. Women: Saree, Salwar Kameez, or traditional modest attire. Shorts and sleeveless tops are not permitted.

Electronics & Cameras

Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode. Photography and video recording inside the inner Garbhagriha sanctum are strictly prohibited.

Offerings & Prasadam

Only pure ghee, flowers, coconuts, and unpeeled fruits may be handed to the temple priests for Archana. No outside cooked food is permitted into the sanctum.
