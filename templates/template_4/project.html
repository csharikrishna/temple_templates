Constructing Our Sacred Landmark: The Ross Creek Sanctuary

Building Regional Victoria's premier Hindu temple complex and community cultural hub at 110 Vaggs Rd, Ross Creek VIC 3351. Featuring a 350-capacity prayer hall, $900k+ Victorian State Government grant funding, and rigorous Working with Children Check (WWCC) child safety standards.

Architectural Specification

Garbhagriha & Maha Mandapam

Carved according to Manasara Shilpa Shastra. Features 24 hand-chiseled granite pillars, brass clad kalasam spires, and sanctums dedicated to Lord Ganesha, Shiva, Vishnu, and Durga.

  • Building Area: 8,500 sq ft
  • Tower Height (Shikhar): 52 Feet (15.8 m)
  • Primary Stone: Kancheepuram Black Granite
  • Current Status: Stone Carving & Foundation Underway
  • Target Completion: Q4 2027
