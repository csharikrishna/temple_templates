// SOCIAL WELFARE & SERVICE

Annadhanam Seva: Nourishing Regional Families

Upholding the sacred Hindu value of Seva (selfless service), BHTCC actively serves western regional Victoria through continuous food security, language preservation, and intercultural dialogue.

Emergency Care Packages & Food Relief

Monthly dry-ration hampers and direct food relief distributed to regional families in need and local crisis accommodation shelters.

Annadhanam Mahaprasad Community Kitchen

Planned commercial community kitchen providing sanctified, nutritious vegetarian meals to all community visitors regardless of background.

Youth Cultural Heritage & Sanskrit Academy

Structured weekend lessons preserving Vedic philosophy, classical Carnatic and Hindustani music, dance, and regional Indian languages.
