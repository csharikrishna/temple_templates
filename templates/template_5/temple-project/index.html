The 5-Acre Masterplan

Synthesizing 2,000 years of traditional Vedic Shilpa Shastra stone architecture with Central Victoria's peaceful eucalyptus landscape and modern ecological engineering.

GatewayZone Spec

The Rajagopuram (Grand Gateway)

Traditional Dravidian & Nagara fusion • Multi-tiered tower

Rising majestically facing East toward the Victorian sunrise. Adorned with intricate sculptural reliefs representing celestial guardians, sacred flora, and cosmic order, welcoming pilgrims into the sanctified perimeter.

Status:Engineering & Architectural Design Complete
AssemblyZone Spec

Maha Mandapam (Pillared Assembly Hall)

Seating for 500+ devotees • Acoustic stone columns

A spacious congregation hall designed for daily community aarti, classical devotional music, Veda parayanam, and sacred festivals. Pillars feature hand-carved motifs reflecting Sanatana Dharma's eternal symbols.

Status:Foundation Engineering Finalized
SanctumZone Spec

Garbhagriha (Sanctum Sanctorum)

Sacred Black Granite • 9x9 Paramasayika Vastu Grid

The inner spiritual sanctum housing Lord Shiva, Lord Ganesha, Lord Murugan, Lord Venkateswara, and Goddess Durga. Handcrafted from heavy south-Indian granite according to rigid Shilpa Shastra stone alignment.

Status:Granite Quarrying & Artisan Carving
SevaZone Spec

Annadhanam Satvik Kitchen & Dining

Commercial community kitchen • 300-seat dining hall

Dedicated to the sacred tradition of offering free sanctified vegetarian nourishment (Mahaprasadam) to all visitors, community members, and vulnerable families across Ballarat.

Status:Services & Plumbing Approved
EducationZone Spec

Cultural & Education Pavilion

4 Multi-purpose Classrooms • Community Library

A center for Sunday Balagokulam, Sanskrit grammar, classical Indian music, Bharatanatyam dance classes, and youth interfaith dialogue.

Status:Phase 2 Planning
LandscapeZone Spec

5-Acre Vastu Meditation Parkland

Native Eucalyptus Trails • Reflection Ponds • Parking

A harmonious synthesis of traditional Hindu sacred landscape design and native Australian bushland. Native vegetation is conserved alongside tranquil walking trails and 150+ parking bays.

Status:Phase 1 Civil Earthworks Active
