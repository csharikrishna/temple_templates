Legal Transparency

Governance & Policies

Ballarat Hindu Temple & Cultural Centre Inc. operates under strict Victorian compliance standards, child safety frameworks, and financial accountability codes.

🏛️ Legal Entity Status

  • Legal Name: Ballarat Hindu Temple & Cultural Centre Incorporated (BHTCC)
  • Registration: Incorporated Association (Victoria, Australia)
  • ABN: 13 397 829 440
  • Charity Subtype: Advancement of Religion / Community Support
  • Tax Status: Registered Charity with Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC)

🛡️ Child Safety Commitment

BHTCC is fully committed to upholding the Victorian Child Safe Standards under the Child Wellbeing and Safety Act 2005.

  • All committee members, instructors, and active volunteers hold current Working with Children Checks (WWCC).
  • Zero-tolerance approach to child abuse or neglect.
  • Clear reporting procedures and safe environment protocols across all cultural classes.
