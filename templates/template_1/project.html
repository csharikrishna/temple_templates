Civil Progress

Construction Milestone Stages

Transparent tracking of civil works at 110 Vaggs Rd, Ross Creek overseen by Project Manager Mr. Ramesh Patel.

✓

Stage 1: Site Acquisition, Surveying & Land Clearance

Completed 2018-2020

Geotechnical soil core testing, flora/fauna ecological study, bushfire risk mitigation, and council road access permits.

✓

Stage 2: Civil Earthworks & In-Ground Utility Grid

Completed 2022-2023

Bulk site leveling, high-capacity septic trenching, electrical conduits, stormwater reticulation, and water pipeline connections.

✓

Stage 3: Reinforced Concrete Foundation Slab

Completed 2024

High-grade engineered concrete slab engineered to support the weight of heavy monolithic granite moolasthanam altars.

⚙

Stage 4: Structural Steel Portal Framework (Active Now)

In Progress (85%)

Erection of heavy galvanized steel portal frames, roof purlins, weather-tight insulation sheeting, and exterior wall cladding.

5

Stage 5: Granite Moolasthanam Stonemasonry

Commencing Q3 2026

Carving and assembling traditional granite blocks for the central Ganesha Garbhagriha, Shiva Lingam peedam, and Ambal shrine.

6

Stage 6: Grand Consecration & Maha Kumbhabhishekam

Target Consecration

Consecration rituals by learned Vedic acharyas, sanctification of kalashas atop the gopuram, and inaugural community feast.
