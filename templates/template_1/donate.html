Australian Tax-Deductibility Notice

BHTCC is an Incorporated Association registered with the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC). Endorsed as a Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR) under Subdivision 30-B of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 (ITAA 1997). Gifts and donations of $2 or more to our building, library, or cultural fund campaigns are fully tax-deductible (ABN: 13 397 829 440). An official DGR receipt is automatically generated upon submission.
