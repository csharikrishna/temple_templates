Milestone Journey

History of the Hindu Community in Ballarat

The journey from informal prayer circles in community halls to regional Victoria's first monumental Hindu Temple and Cultural Centre.

2016
FOUNDATION MILESTONE

Formal Incorporation of BHTCC

Ballarat Hindu Temple & Cultural Centre Incorporated formally registered as a Victorian not-for-profit association to unite regional diaspora families across Western Victoria.

Victorian Reg. NFP • ABN: 13 397 829 440
2018
LAND ACQUISITION

Ross Creek 5-Acre Sanctuary Acquired

Acquired picturesque 5-acre freehold sanctuary at 110 Vaggs Rd, Ross Creek. Initiated geotechnical soil surveys, flora/fauna ecological assessments, and community masterplanning.

5 Acres Freehold • Consecrated Bhoomi
2020
STATUTORY APPROVALS

Parliamentary Recognition & Council Permits

Secured formal planning permits from Golden Plains Shire and bipartisan support in the Victorian Parliament for the regional cultural centre.

INFRASTRUCTURE FUNDING

Funding formalized under the Regional Infrastructure & Multicultural Facilities programs to commence extensive civil earthworks, road access infrastructure, and utility lines.
